The Problem

Inter-store transfers are usually an afterthought.

Parts get shuttled between locations by whoever's free — a manager's personal car, an ad-hoc courier, or a route that only sort-of runs on schedule. No visibility, no accountability, no consistency store to store.

The Solution

A dedicated fleet, run on your route, your schedule.

Routed contracts directly with you to run fixed routes between your stores, multiple times a day if needed — with live tracking and proof of delivery built in from day one, not bolted on later.

How It Works

From contract to delivered, in four steps.

Route & contract setup

We map your stores, stop sequence, and frequency, and agree on a performance-based rate structure.

Scheduled pickup

A dedicated vehicle — matched to what you're actually shipping — arrives on your route's fixed schedule.

Live delivery

Track the vehicle and what's loaded on it in real time as it runs the route.

Proof & reporting

Every drop is logged and timestamped — look it up anytime through the delivery portal.

The Fleet

Consistent vehicle types, matched to the route.

Every route runs the same vehicle class every time — so you know exactly what will and won't fit before you place the order.

Cargo Route

Cybertruck

Open-bed capacity for oversized or bulk items that don't fit a standard route vehicle — drums, long parts, larger orders.

Standard Route

Model Y

The default route vehicle for regular parts volume — enclosed cargo space, all-electric, Level 2 driver-assist.

Light Route

Model 3

For lower-volume, higher-frequency routes where speed between stops matters more than cargo space.

About Routed

A Marietta-based fleet, built for Georgia retail.

Routed (Tend To Solutions LLC) runs an all-electric delivery fleet dedicated to fixed-route parts and freight transfers for multi-location retail chains across Georgia. No passengers, no ride-hailing — just reliable, tracked, on-schedule delivery between your stores.

Why an EV fleet

Level 2 driver-assist reduces driver fatigue and improves safety on repeat routes. No gas means no fuel-surcharge line item — your contracted rate is your rate.
